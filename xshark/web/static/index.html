Fill quality by leg

Does each leg buy below what the share turns out to be worth? Markout = the side's book mid 1 s / 5 s / 30 s later minus the price paid (in cents a share); positive is good for us. The trader's resting bids won +1.2 points over their price; a resting bid that only fills when the ask falls through it shows up here as a negative markout.
